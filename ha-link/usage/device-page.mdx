A device page brings together the entities Home Assistant knows about for one device.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your device">
    Go to **Settings → Devices & services → Devices**. Search for the device and select its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check its controls and readings">
    Look through the available controls and sensors. Select an entity to open its details; the contents depend on the device and integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a room">
    Return to the **Devices** list and select the device in selection mode. Select **Move to area** and choose its room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an area if needed">
    If the room is missing, open **Settings → Areas, labels & zones → Create area**. Give it a name, select **Create**, then assign your device to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it in HA Link">
    Return to **HA Link**, select the refresh icon at the top (**Refresh connections**), and search for the device's entities. They appear when selected for HA Link and are not hidden or disabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>You know where to find your device's entities and which room it belongs to.</Check>

For room organization, see the [official areas guide](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/organizing/areas/).
