**Your entities** lists the controls and readings provided by your connected integrations. One physical device can have several entities.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an entity">
    Open **HA Link** and scroll to **Your entities**. Type a name or entity ID, such as `light.kitchen`, into **Search by name or entity ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Use **All routes** to choose a connection, or **Additional entities** when that option appears. Use **All states**, **Available**, or **Unavailable / unknown** to filter by status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    Each row shows the entity's name, ID, connection, and current state. Select a row to open its Home Assistant details and any supported controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear filters if something is missing">
    Clear the search and return to **All routes** and **All states**. After setting up a connection, select the refresh icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
The result count shows how many entities match your filters. Hidden and disabled entities are excluded from HA Link.
</Check>
