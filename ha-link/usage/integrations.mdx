Home Assistant's **Devices & services** page is where you manage the integrations that connect your devices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Devices & services">
    In Home Assistant, open **Settings → Devices & services**. The Integrations tab lists your configured connections and any discovered devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a connection">
    Select an integration such as **Google Cast**, **Matter**, or **HA Link**. From HA Link, **Manage devices** on a connected route also opens its native integration page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View its devices and entities">
    Open the integration's device or entity list, then select the item you want. An entity is one control or reading, such as a light or temperature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add another connection">
    Return to **Devices & services**, select **Add integration**, and search for the provider. Complete its setup prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to HA Link">
    Open **HA Link** and select the refresh icon at the top (**Refresh connections**) after finishing setup. Selected routes show their entities, excluding hidden or disabled ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>You can find each integration and open the devices or entities it provides.</Check>

For more detail, see Home Assistant's [integration guide](https://www.home-assistant.io/getting-started/integration/).
