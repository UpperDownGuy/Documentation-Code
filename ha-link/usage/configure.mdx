Change HA Link's settings whenever you want to show different routes or include entities from another integration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open HA Link's integration page">
    Select **Configure** at the top of the HA Link sidebar page. This opens the integration's page under **Settings → Devices & services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options">
    Open the HA Link entry's settings using its options or configure control. The form is titled **HA Link connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to show">
    Select the **Connection routes** you want in the workspace. Use **Other existing entities** to add entities already available in Home Assistant, such as ones from a manufacturer's integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your selection">
    Keep at least one route or existing entity selected, then submit the form. Return to **HA Link** and refresh to check your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
HA Link shows your selected connections and entities. Removing a route here leaves its native integration and devices connected.
</Check>

<Note>
Route selection changes the workspace. Pairing and account sign-in happen through the native integration's setup.
</Note>
