Use this page for supported Echo, Fire, and other devices with Alexa built in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Alexa card">
    Open **HA Link** in the Home Assistant sidebar and find **Alexa devices**. Select **Set up**, or **Manage devices** if you already connected your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your Amazon account">
    Enable two-step verification with an authenticator app and make it your preferred method. Have a fresh verification code ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Alexa Devices setup">
    Follow Home Assistant's sign-in prompts. Enter your account details and verification code in that native setup window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh HA Link">
    Return to **HA Link** and select the refresh icon at the top (**Refresh connections**). Choose **Alexa devices** in the route filter to find its entities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a control">
    Select an entity to open its Home Assistant details. The available controls depend on your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>Your supported Alexa devices are connected, with their available entities shown in HA Link.</Check>

<Note>This connection does not import every third-party accessory listed in the Alexa app.</Note>

For sign-in help, see the [official Alexa Devices guide](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/alexa_devices/).
