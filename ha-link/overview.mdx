## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install HA Link" icon="download">
    Follow the [installation guide](/download) to add HA Link to Home Assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your connections" icon="plug">
    Complete [integration onboarding](/integration-onboarding), then review your [connection routes](/ha-link/usage/connection-routes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your workspace" icon="house-plug">
    Select **HA Link** in the Home Assistant sidebar to see your connections and [find your entities](/ha-link/usage/entities).
  </Step>
</Steps>
