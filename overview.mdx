## From an idea to your first integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your developer account" icon="user-round">
    Open the [developer console](https://api.alexxweb.com/developers) and create a separate account for your apps, credentials, and data. Follow the [account guide](/v1/developer-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your app access" icon="key">
    Create an [API key](/v1/api-keys) with the permissions your app needs, or connect another user's account through [OAuth](/v1/oauth).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build something useful" icon="blocks">
    [Store a resource](/v1/resources), listen for [events](/v1/events), or connect your data to [Home Assistant](/v1/home-assistant).
  </Step>
</Steps>
